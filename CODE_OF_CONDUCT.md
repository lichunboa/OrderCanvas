# Community code of conduct

Our open-source spaces are for discussing reproducible technical problems, useful changes and respectful collaboration around MQL5 tools and indicators.

Participants should:
- Be constructive and specific when disagreeing; critique code and behavior, not people.
- Respect authorship, open-source licenses and source attribution.
- Avoid spam, repeated unsolicited advertising, market manipulation, threats, harassment, hate speech or exposing other people's private information.
- Never post brokerage credentials, activation keys, payment data or private trading records.
- Keep issues on-topic. For commercial purchase and account-specific support, use the support route in the product README, not public technical Issues.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions that violate these expectations and restrict participation in serious or repeated cases.

For reporting unacceptable conduct, first contact maintainers privately using the support route in the product README without including personal or sensitive details in public Issues. For security-sensitive material use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
